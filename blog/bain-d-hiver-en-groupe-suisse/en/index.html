Winter swimming in a group: where to start with company in Switzerland

By the My Lake team · published 15 November 2026

In short

To start, the safest way is to go with others: Graubünden Ferien says so, Helsana too, and Zürich Tourismus recommends guidance for beginners. Here is what we could verify: Eisbadi Arosa (daily workshop for beginners), courses in Davos and St. Moritz, guided courses at Seebad Utoquai in Zurich. We found no official list of winter-swimming clubs in Switzerland, and we do not invent one.

Why go in a group?

Graubünden Ferien writes that ice bathing is done only with company: it is more fun in a group, and safer if your circulation gives out despite preparation. Helsana asks you to bring a second person, for safety, and the Swiss Lifesaving Society reminds us of the minutes rule: as many minutes as the water has degrees. Zürich Tourismus recommends a guided offer for beginners, which provides the basics and some bearings.

Graubünden: near-freezing water, with guidance

  • Eisbadi Arosa: presented as the first Eisbadi in Switzerland, with a daily workshop for beginners; the bathing spots are then open at any time to experienced swimmers, and a sauna wagon lets you warm up (Graubünden Ferien).
  • Davos: Markus Blum offers courses for beginners and advanced swimmers, in a pool by Lake Davos, or for the experienced in a hole cut in the ice of the natural lake (Graubünden Ferien).
  • St. Moritz: you can bathe in the frozen lake behind the Reithalle, with changing rooms at the Eisarena Ludains; for beginners, St. Moritz Outdoors offers guided ice bathing (Graubünden Ferien).

Zurich: courses and sauna

According to Zürich Tourismus, Seebad Utoquai offers structured introductions, with courses led by professionals. The city also pairs winter swimming with sauna: the Sauna am Fluss alternates heat with short cold dips in the Limmat, and the Saunaboot lets you swim in Lake Zurich after the sauna, in private. Our guides: winter swimming in Zurich, and the lakeside saunas.

Lake Geneva and the Three Lakes

We cover Geneva and Lausanne in winter swimming on Lake Geneva, and Neuchâtel, Biel and Murten in winter swimming in the Three Lakes. To warm up afterwards: the saunas of Lake Geneva.

What we could not verify

We found no official list of winter-swimming clubs or groups in Switzerland, and Graubünden Ferien cites an umbrella body, the “Swiss Cold Training Association”, whose pages we did not read. If you run a regular outing and would like it mentioned, write to us: we will check it before publishing.

Disclaimer. This article gives general information taken from the sources cited; it is not medical advice. Cold-water swimming puts real strain on the heart and can be dangerous: talk to a doctor before you start, especially with a heart condition, high blood pressure or during pregnancy. Never swim alone and follow lifeguards’ instructions. You swim at your own risk; in an emergency, call 144 in Switzerland or 112.
