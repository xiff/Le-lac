Where to swim in Lake Constance: every beach

By the My Lake team · published 1 November 2026 · updated 13 November 2026

In short

My Lake follows 12 beaches on Lake Constance, with the water temperature 70 cm below the surface where it is calculated. Here is the list, then useful places around the water.

Lake Constance covers about 473 km², is up to 251 m deep and lies at 395 m above sea level. Its shores are shared by Switzerland, Germany and Austria.

Entry, season and access

Prices and dates collected on 13 November 2026 from the sources below, for the 2026 season: check each baths’ website before you go.

  • Strandbad Buchhorn, Arbon: the town’s 2026 prices, adult entry CHF 8, CHF 4 for children and pupils; open 10am to 7pm until 7 June, then 9am to 8pm from 8 June to 9 August (badi-info.ch).
  • Seebad Romanshorn: adult entry CHF 10 since the increase (it was CHF 8), CHF 5 for children.

A drink or a meal by Lake Constance

  • Strandbad Buchhorn, Arbon: Slightly raised terrace facing the lake; the Swiss Heimatschutz ranks this bath among the most beautiful in Switzerland.
  • Restaurant Hafen, Romanshorn: Right on the water in Romanshorn’s harbour basin.
  • Restaurant Fischerhaus, Kreuzlingen: Relaxed beer garden right on the lakeshore.
  • Restaurant Schiff, Altnau: On the shore with a view over the lake: in summer, a large terrace right on Lake Constance.
  • Krone am See, Altnau: By the harbour basin; you can order online from the beach or your boat. Reduced hours in winter.
  • Restaurant Schiff Kesswil, Kesswil: Right on Lake Constance, with a lake view and a fondue room.

Saunas on Lake Constance

Each entry links to the operator’s own website when there is one. Hours and prices change: check with them.

  • Wagensauna, Romanshorn (et rives voisines), near Romanshorn: Mobile winter sauna on the Thurgau shore of Lake Constance (Romanshorn, Staad, Kesswil, Goldach, Bottighofen): 9am to 10pm, from CHF 30 an hour for 1 to 2 people. Sites change: see the website.
