Where to swim in Lausanne and Morges

By the My Lake team · published 21 November 2026

In short

Between Saint-Prex and Pully, My Lake follows 10 beaches: Morges, Préverenges, Vidy, Ouchy, Bellerive and more. The page gives the morning water temperature, places by the water and rental operators.

These are the beaches around Lausanne and Morges followed by the app, with the morning temperature when the model has one.

Where to have a drink or eat by the water

  • Le Pavois, Morges: Restaurant of the Mont-Blanc au Lac hotel, terrace on the Morges quays.
  • La Jetée de la Compagnie, Lausanne (May to September): Summer bar on the Ouchy jetty: deck, barbecue and sunsets.
  • Hôtel-Restaurant de la Plage, Préverenges: At the end of Avenue de la Plage, sunny terrace a stone’s throw from the big sandy beach.
  • La Guinguette de Vidy, Lausanne (April to September): In front of the Vidy Bowl by the water: where walkers and paddlers meet.
  • Le Lacustre, Lausanne: Above the Ouchy pier: wood-fired pizza and weekend brunch.
  • Bar du Beau-Rivage Palace, Lausanne: The fancy drink, terrace under the arches facing the lake and the Alps.
  • La Générale, Pully (April to September): Pully harbour bar, stone steps down to the lake, beach volleyball next door.
  • Restaurant du Port de Pully, Pully: On the harbour, facing the lake and the Alps.

Renting on the water or warming up in a sauna

Each entry links to the operator’s own website when there is one. Hours and prices change: check with them.

  • Passion Nautique, Morges, near Morges: Rental base in Morges, from pedal boats to licence-free motorboats.
  • Pedalino, Lausanne (May to September), near Lausanne – Vidy: Summer water-sports centre at the east end of Vidy beach: paddles, pedal boats, motorboats.
  • École de voile d’Ouchy, Lausanne, near Lausanne – Ouchy: Sailing taster trips on Lake Geneva for families and groups, half or full day.
  • Pieds dans l’O, Lausanne, near Lausanne – Ouchy: At Ouchy’s old harbour: pedal boats, electric or motorboats, wakeboard and water skiing.
  • Bains des Rives, Lausanne (October to March), near Lausanne – Bellerive: At Bellerive, wood-fired yurt saunas and a lakeside terrace, with a dip in Lake Geneva between sessions, October to March.
  • Ski Nautique Club Lausanne, Lausanne, near Lausanne – Ouchy: Ouchy club since 1966, Tour Haldimand pier: water-skiing, wakeboard and wakesurf lessons open to non-members.

Before you dive in: check the water quality. In winter: winter swimming on Lake Geneva. The current wind and water are in the My Lake app.
