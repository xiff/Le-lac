Cafés and restaurants on the shores of Lake Geneva, region by region

By the My Lake team · published 20 October 2026 · updated 27 October 2026

In short

My Lake lists 34 cafés and restaurants on the shores of Lake Geneva, checked against tourist offices and the places’ own websites: from the Bains des Pâquis in Geneva to the Vevey waterfront, from the Guinguette de Vidy to Sciez beach. Many beach cafés only open in the warm season: the period is shown when known.

Where can you eat or drink by Lake Geneva?

Geneva

  • Buvette des Bains des Pâquis, Genève: Open all year: fondue in winter, dish of the day in summer, feet almost in the water.
  • Tropical Corner, Cologny (April to October): Lakeside watersports club since 1981: terrace under palm trees, burgers and tapas, wakeboard pontoon, windsurf and paddle.
  • Restaurant de la Plage & Buvette, Genève: On stilts at the end of Eaux-Vives beach: perch, hot dogs and ice cream.
  • La Barje des Lavandières, Genève (May to September): Summer bar on the Rhône with free concerts, a stone’s throw from the harbour.
  • La Perle du Lac, Genève: Pavilion in Mon Repos park, big terrace facing the lake. Closed Mondays in winter.
  • Hôtel du Parc des Eaux-Vives, Genève: Mansion at the top of the park, lake and Jura views. For a special occasion.
  • Auberge d’Hermance, Hermance: Lakeside village 20 minutes from Geneva, local produce.

La Côte

  • Hôtel du Lac, Coppet: Terrace with a lake view, between Geneva and Nyon.
  • Le Casino, Rolle: Shaded terrace on the lakeside promenade and a bright veranda. Closed Sunday evening and Monday.
  • O’Les Terrasses du Lac, Nyon: Rue de Rive, three spaces and a terrace facing the lake and the Alps, from morning coffee to the last drink.
  • Restaurant de la Plage, Nyon: Lake fish and regional dishes by the water, Sunday brunch.

Morges & Lausanne

  • Le Pavois, Morges: Restaurant of the Mont-Blanc au Lac hotel, terrace on the Morges quays.
  • La Jetée de la Compagnie, Lausanne (May to September): Summer bar on the Ouchy jetty: deck, barbecue and sunsets.
  • Hôtel-Restaurant de la Plage, Préverenges: At the end of Avenue de la Plage, sunny terrace a stone’s throw from the big sandy beach.
  • La Guinguette de Vidy, Lausanne (April to September): In front of the Vidy Bowl by the water: where walkers and paddlers meet.
  • Le Lacustre, Lausanne: Above the Ouchy pier: wood-fired pizza and weekend brunch.
  • Bar du Beau-Rivage Palace, Lausanne: The fancy drink, terrace under the arches facing the lake and the Alps.
  • La Générale, Pully (April to September): Pully harbour bar, stone steps down to the lake, beach volleyball next door.
  • Restaurant du Port de Pully, Pully: On the harbour, facing the lake and the Alps.
  • Le Rivage, Lutry: Terrace on the Lutry quays, lake fish, at the foot of Lavaux.

Riviera

  • Hôtel des Trois Couronnes, Vevey: Belle Époque panoramic terrace on the Vevey quays.
  • Kju, Vevey: Restaurant, bar and club in an 18th-century boathouse, one of the finest terraces in Vevey.
  • La Véranda (Grand Hôtel du Lac), Vevey: Terrace over the harbour, the lake and the Alps, relaxed cooking.
  • La Terrasse du Petit Palais, Montreux (May to September): The Montreux Palace terrace, facing the lake and the Alps, in fine weather.
  • Le Phare, Le Bouveret: Lake terrace facing the sunset, open 9am to midnight.
  • Café Helvetia « Au 17 17 », Saint-Gingolph: Big terrace above the lake, open all year round.
  • Restaurant Bellevue, Saint-Gingolph: On the quay, terrace facing the lake and the mountains.

French shore

  • Bar-Restaurant de la Plage municipale, Thonon-les-Bains (May to September): In the middle of the town beach on Quai de Ripaille: dish of the day and local specialities, feet in the water.
  • Hôtel Restaurant de la Plage, Excenevex: Feet in the water all year round, lake and mountain views, seasonal home cooking.
  • La Guinguette de la plage, Excenevex: The beach bar on Excenevex’s big sandy beach.
  • Restaurant Plage Les Pêcheurs, Sciez: An institution since 1964: feet-in-the-water terrace under century-old plane trees.
  • Yacht Restaurant, Évian-les-Bains: At the Hilton on Quai Paul-Léger, Mediterranean food facing the lake.
  • Ô Lac, Neuvecelle: Hotel restaurant at Grande Rive, panoramic terrace facing the lake.
  • Hôtel-Restaurant du Port, Yvoire (March to October): Medieval village, terrace on the harbour on the French side.

Are beach cafés open all year?

No: many only open from April or May to September, depending on the weather. The seasons above come from tourist offices or the places’ websites; hours and seasons change, so a phone call before you go avoids disappointment. The “Go out” tab in the app shows “Closed until…” out of season.

Is a place missing?

The app’s “Go out” tab has a short form to suggest a place, reviewed before it is added. To pick a nearby beach, see the beach list and, in Geneva, all the canton’s beaches.
