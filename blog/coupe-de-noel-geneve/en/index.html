The 2026 Coupe de Noël in Geneva: 100 metres in the harbour in December

By the My Lake team · published 15 November 2026

In short

The 88th Coupe de Noël takes place on 19 and 20 December 2026 in Geneva harbour: 100 metres from the Jardin Anglais, in timed heats or in fancy dress. Entry costs CHF 45, and the official website says the edition is full. Watching is free.

What is the Coupe de Noël?

A 100-metre race in the lake, in midwinter, in the heart of Geneva. It began in 1934: a member of the Cercle des Nageurs de Genève, Mr Doria, created it after a race he had seen on the Seine in Paris, with fewer than ten swimmers in the first year. The organiser describes it as the canton’s oldest sporting event, listed as Swiss intangible heritage in 2023. Today it is run by the Association CdN 1934, with support from the City of Geneva’s sports department.

When and where?

On Saturday 19 and Sunday 20 December 2026. The start is on the left bank of the harbour, at the Swissboat pontoon by the Jardin Anglais, near the Quai Marchand; the finish is about 100 metres downstream. The first start is at 8.30 am, then a wave leaves every 5 minutes; for safety there are never more than two waves (50 swimmers) in the water at once.

Which categories?

  • Timed heats (500 places): ranked by age and sex, on Sunday morning between 8.30 and noon. New in 2026: a 70 and over category and the Gaëtan Beysard Trophy, with a 100 m wave open to everyone on Sunday at noon.
  • Fun heats (4,000 places): in fancy dress, untimed and unranked. A costume must never get in the way of swimming: Geneva’s lake rescue, the navigation police and the organiser may refuse a start or take out of the water anyone who cannot make progress. No confetti.
  • The Race, the elite race: Sunday 20 December at 10 am for men and 10.30 am for women, with cash prizes for the top five.

All categories are open to swimmers born in 2011 or earlier, i.e. aged 15 and over.

Entry and price

Entry costs CHF 45 per person (EUR 48), up from CHF 40 for more than a decade. It opened in two stages: 26 September 2026 for timed heats and 10 October for fun heats, paid by card or Twint. On 15 November 2026 the official website says the 88th edition is full.

On the day

  • Changing rooms: on the boat “Le Genève” at the Quai Marchand, heated but without showers, open from 7.45 am. The organiser suggests arriving an hour before your wave.
  • Call room: a few steps from the changing rooms, about 15 minutes before your wave. You can leave a robe, towel and sandals there.
  • Out of the water: a hot tea, a souvenir gift and a wood-fired hot tub; fondue, raclette and hot drinks in the event village.
  • Getting there: about 20 minutes on foot from Cornavin station, 15 minutes from Eaux-Vives station; stops Métropole, Place des Eaux-Vives, Rive and Molard. The organiser asks you to use public transport.

How to prepare?

The organiser is clear: cold-water swimming cannot be improvised. Its advice: build up gradually, never swim alone, wear a cap, enter the water slowly and wait for your breathing to settle before swimming, know your limits, warm up gradually afterwards without a very hot shower, and get out if you feel disoriented, shiver uncontrollably or lose feeling.
