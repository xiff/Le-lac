The lake makes its own climate: softening winter, cooling summer

By the My Lake team · published 17 November 2026

In short

Through the mass of water it holds, Lake Geneva creates a microclimate: in winter, the lake gives back the heat stored over summer, which softens temperatures nearby; in summer, it cools its whole shoreline. Montreux and its surroundings are sheltered from the bise.

A great volume of water

Located on the north-western edge of the Alps, Lake Geneva holds an enormous mass of water, with a mean depth of 152.7 m. This mass changes temperature slowly, far more slowly than air.

Winter and summer

According to Wikipedia, in winter the lake gives back the heat stored over summer, which softens temperatures nearby; in summer, it cools its whole shoreline. This is also why the water stays pleasant long after summer, and cold long after winter.

Montreux, sheltered from the bise

The part of the Vaud Riviera around Montreux is sheltered from the bise, the cold north-easterly: the winds of Lake Geneva.
