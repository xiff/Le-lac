Where to swim in Lake Lucerne: every beach

By the My Lake team · published 3 November 2026 · updated 13 November 2026

In short

My Lake follows 18 beaches on Lake Lucerne, with the water temperature 70 cm below the surface where it is calculated. Here is the list, then useful places around the water.

Lake Lucerne covers about 113.6 km², is up to 214 m deep and lies at 434 m above sea level.

Entry, season and access

Prices and dates collected on 13 November 2026 from the sources below, for the 2026 season: check each baths’ website before you go.

  • Seebad Luzern: open from 1 May, season normally until Bettag (20 September 2026), closed in bad weather; adult entry CHF 8, CHF 4 for ages 6 to 15.
  • Lido Luzern (a separate, paid facility): adult entry CHF 8 on weekdays and CHF 10 at weekends; CHF 5 under 16, free under 6.

A drink or a meal by Lake Lucerne

  • Seebad Luzern, Lucerne: Open-air bar and bistro in the historic baths in the city centre; the restaurant stays open in the evening after the baths close at 8pm.
  • Seerestaurant Lido, Lucerne: Terrace and bar by the lake at Lucerne’s Lido; according to the directory, open Wednesday to Sunday.
  • Lido Weggis, Weggis: Bath built in 1919, with a restaurant and a summer terrace facing the lake.
  • Seerestaurant Lützelau, Weggis: Front row on the water, with a terrace and bar.
  • Quai 2, Brunnen: Café, restaurant and bar on the lake promenade, plus the Lakeside, a terrace right on the water.
  • Fischli am See, Bauen: Waterside terrace on the Swiss Path.
  • Seerestaurant Schlüssel, Ennetbürgen: Pizzeria and restaurant with a terrace right on the lake and its own boat mooring.

Renting on Lake Lucerne

Each entry links to the operator’s own website when there is one. Hours and prices change: check with them.

  • Saunaboot s’Berti, Lucerne (Kastanienbaum), near Lucerne – Lido: Sauna boat on Lake Lucerne, moored in the harbour at the foot of the Seehotel Kastanienbaum.
