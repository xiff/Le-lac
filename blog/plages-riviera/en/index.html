Where to swim on the Vaud and Valais Riviera

By the My Lake team · published 22 November 2026

In short

From Corseaux to Saint-Gingolph, via Vevey, La Tour-de-Peilz, Montreux, Villeneuve and Le Bouveret, My Lake follows 9 beaches. The page gives the morning water temperature, places by the water and rental operators.

These are the Riviera beaches followed by the app. The Lavaux beaches (Lutry, Villette, Cully, Rivaz, Saint-Saphorin) have their own page.

Where to have a drink or eat by the water

  • Hôtel des Trois Couronnes, Vevey: Belle Époque panoramic terrace on the Vevey quays.
  • Kju, Vevey: Restaurant, bar and club in an 18th-century boathouse, one of the finest terraces in Vevey.
  • La Véranda (Grand Hôtel du Lac), Vevey: Terrace over the harbour, the lake and the Alps, relaxed cooking.
  • La Terrasse du Petit Palais, Montreux (May to August): The Montreux Palace terrace, facing the lake and the Alps, in fine weather.
  • Le Phare, Le Bouveret: Lake terrace facing the sunset, open 9am to midnight.
  • Café Helvetia « Au 17 17 », Saint-Gingolph: Big terrace above the lake, open all year round.
  • Restaurant Bellevue, Saint-Gingolph: On the quay, terrace facing the lake and the mountains.

Renting on the water or warming up in a sauna

Each entry links to the operator’s own website when there is one. Hours and prices change: check with them.

  • SwissPaddle, Corseaux, near Corseaux: Paddle boards and wing foil in Corseaux, next to Nestlé in Vevey.
  • Nautic Loisirs, Montreux (April to August), near Montreux – Clarens: Quai des Fleurs: paddles, kayaks, windsurf boards and motorboats.
  • Evolution 2 Saint-Gingolph, Saint-Gingolph (June to September), near Saint-Gingolph: Water-sports base on the town beach: solo or tandem paddle, kayak, e-foil, June to September, weather permitting.
  • Water Sports Montreux, Montreux, near Montreux – Clarens: One of Switzerland’s oldest watersports clubs, pier below the Montreux Casino: wakeboard, wakesurf and water-skiing, gear included.
  • Steam on the Water, Petit Port Eiffel, Vevey (October to April), near Vevey: Sauna set up at the Petit Port Eiffel with a direct lake view, October to April.
  • Steam on the Water, la Maladaire, La Tour-de-Peilz (November to April), near La Tour-de-Peilz: At the Maladaire campsite (Route de Saint-Maurice 310): wood-fired sauna wagon on the shore, a rest yurt and a barrel sauna for private groups. Early November 2026 to mid-April 2027, 9am–10pm (from 3pm on Wednesdays and Thursdays). CHF 20, CHF 18 reduced, booked online.

Before you dive in: check the water quality. In winter: winter swimming on Lake Geneva. The current wind and water are in the My Lake app.
