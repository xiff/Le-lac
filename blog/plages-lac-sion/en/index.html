Where to swim at the Domaine des Îles (Sion): every beach

By the My Lake team · published 7 November 2026 · updated 13 November 2026

In short

My Lake follows one beach at the Domaine des Îles (Sion): Domaine des Îles, with the water temperature 70 cm below the surface where it is calculated. Here is the list, then useful places around the water.

The Domaine des Îles is a 53.5-hectare estate on the Rhône, with a lake that began as a former gravel pit.

The beach followed at the Domaine des Îles (Sion)

  • Domaine des Îles

The water is not measured here: no model calculates it yet, and the app prefers not to make up a figure.

Entry, season and access

Prices and dates collected on 13 November 2026 from the sources below, for the 2026 season: check each baths’ website before you go.

  • The estate, run by the Bourgeoisie of Sion, is described as free and open all year (Discovr).
  • Lifeguard-supervised swimming is announced mainly from May to September (Alpvision, a page about a year old): I have no confirmation for 2026, ask the Bourgeoisie.

A drink or a meal at the Domaine des Îles (Sion)

  • Restaurant des Îles, Sion: At the Domaine des Îles by the lake: restaurant with a sunny terrace.

Renting on Sion – Domaine des Îles

Each entry links to the operator’s own website when there is one. Hours and prices change: check with them.

  • Premier Alpine Centre, Sion, near Domaine des Îles: Stand-up paddleboard rental at the Domaine des Îles, every day in summer from 1pm to 6pm according to the Passeport valaisan.
  • Centre nautique du Domaine des Îles, Sion (May to September), near Domaine des Îles: In summer, pedal boats, kayaks and stand-up paddleboards to rent, with lessons for beginners; mostly May to September.
