Paddleboarding on Lake Geneva: when to go and where to rent

By the My Lake team · published 1 November 2026 · updated 4 November 2026

In short

For paddleboarding, gusts are what matter. In My Lake, paddleboarding is “Perfect” up to 12 km/h of gusts, “Good” up to 20 km/h, “Meh” up to 28 km/h and not advised beyond; rain or air below 8 °C costs one notch. When the bise blows, stay close to shore or wait.

What is the maximum wind for paddleboarding?

Standing on a board, you catch a lot of wind, and waves quickly throw you off balance. These are the app’s guidelines (not official standards):

  • Perfect: gusts up to 12 km/h, the lake is like glass.
  • Good: up to 20 km/h, a few ripples.
  • Meh: up to 28 km/h, it gets choppy.
  • No: beyond that, or in a thunderstorm.

Rain or air below 8 °C costs one notch. Today’s wind for each beach is in the app; the bise deserves special attention.

Where can you rent a paddleboard on Lake Geneva?

  • Tropical Corner, Cologny (April to October), near Tropical Corner: Windsurf school since 1981: board and paddle rental, wakeboard pontoon.
  • SUP Club Nyon, Nyon, near Nyon: Lakeside paddle club on Rue de Rive.
  • Pedalino, Lausanne (May to September), near Lausanne – Vidy: Summer water-sports centre at the east end of Vidy beach: paddles, pedal boats, motorboats.
  • Lutry’s Paddle School, Lutry, near Lutry: Paddle boards and kayaks to rent in summer on Curtinaux beach, in the village centre.
  • SwissPaddle, Corseaux, near Corseaux: Paddle boards and wing foil in Corseaux, next to Nestlé in Vevey.
  • Nautic Loisirs, Montreux, near Montreux – Clarens: Quai des Fleurs: paddles, kayaks, windsurf boards and motorboats.
  • Evolution 2 Saint-Gingolph, Saint-Gingolph (June to September), near Saint-Gingolph: Water-sports base on the town beach: solo or tandem paddle, kayak, e-surf, June to September, weather permitting.
  • Centre nautique d’Évian, Évian (July to August), near Évian: Town base on Avenue du Général-Dupas: rowing boats, pedal boats and paddles, July and August afternoons.
  • Evolution 2 Thonon, Thonon, near Thonon: Water-sports base on Quai de Ripaille near Pinède beach: paddle, giant paddle, kayak, pedal boat.

Prices, hours and availability change: check with the rental place before you go.

Which beach is best for beginners?

A beach with easy access and shelter from the day’s wind: the wind arrow on each beach page helps you pick the least exposed shore.
