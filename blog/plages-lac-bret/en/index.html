Where to swim in Lac de Bret: every beach

By the My Lake team · published 5 November 2026 · updated 13 November 2026

In short

My Lake follows one beach on Lac de Bret: Lac de Bret, with the water temperature 70 cm below the surface where it is calculated. Here is the list, then useful places around the water.

Lac de Bret lies at 674 m above sea level.

Entry, season and access

Prices and dates collected on 13 November 2026 from the sources below, for the 2026 season: check each baths’ website before you go.

  • Lac de Bret is a drinking-water reserve and a sensitive natural site: no developed public bathing area appears in the official sources consulted.
  • A classification project published in 2025 by the Canton of Vaud plans to ban swimming and watercraft in the protected northern part of the lake, so as not to disturb wildlife. We could not confirm whether it has come into force: check locally before swimming.

A drink or a meal by Lac de Bret

  • Restaurant du Lac de Bret, Puidoux: An institution on the shore of little Lac de Bret: brasserie, finer cooking and a shaded terrace.
