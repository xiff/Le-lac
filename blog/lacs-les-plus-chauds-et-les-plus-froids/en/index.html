Which Swiss lakes are the warmest, and the coldest?

By the My Lake team · published 16 November 2026

In short

The 25 lakes in My Lake are not at the same temperature at the same time: the gap shows up each morning in the Alplakes (Eawag) model data. This page shows the day’s ranking, with its date; it changes every day.

This morning’s ranking

Average of each lake’s beaches, morning temperature of 19 November 2026 (Alplakes model, Eawag). It changes every day.

Warmest

  • Lake Maggiore : 14.6 °C
  • Lake Zurich : 13.4 °C
  • Lake Lugano : 13.2 °C
  • Lake Gruyère : 12.6 °C
  • Lake Thun : 11.7 °C

Coldest

  • Lake Silvaplana : 5.8 °C
  • Lake Sarnen : 9.4 °C
  • Lac de Joux : 9.6 °C
  • Lake Geneva : 9.7 °C
  • Lac de Bret : 9.8 °C

How to read this ranking

Each value is the average, for a lake, of the model temperature at its beaches in the morning. It is a model estimate, at about 70 cm below the surface: it can differ from what you feel on a sunny afternoon. Lakes without a model (Domaine des Îles in Sion) are not listed.

For each lake

Each lake’s page gives its beaches, their morning temperature and the basic figures (area, depth, altitude): see, for example, the temperature of Lake Geneva. The full list is in all beaches.

The current wind, water temperature and bise, beach by beach, are in the My Lake app.
