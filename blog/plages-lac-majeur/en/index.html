Where to swim in Lake Maggiore: every beach

By the My Lake team · published 2 November 2026 · updated 13 November 2026

In short

My Lake follows 7 beaches on Lake Maggiore, with the water temperature 70 cm below the surface where it is calculated. Here is the list, then useful places around the water.

Lake Maggiore covers about 212.5 km², is up to 372 m deep and lies at 193 m above sea level. Its shores are shared by Switzerland and Italy.

The 7 beaches followed on Lake Maggiore

In brackets: the morning temperature on 13 November 2026, 70 cm below the surface (Alplakes model).

  • Brissago (15.5 °C)
  • Ascona (15.5 °C)
  • Gerra (Gambarogno) (15.5 °C)

  • Tenero (15.5 °C)

Entry, season and access

Prices and dates collected on 13 November 2026 from the sources below, for the 2026 season: check each baths’ website before you go.

  • Lido Locarno: summer 2026 prices: pools CHF 13 for an adult, pools and water slides CHF 18; CHF 7 for children aged 3 to 15 (pools), free under 3. Hours change through the season: see the lido’s website.

A drink or a meal by Lake Maggiore

  • Ristorante Lago Maggiore, Tenero: Via Lido 2, near Tenero’s Lido.
  • Delta Beach Lounge, Ascona: Recently renovated Bauhaus-style building: large terrace, spacious garden and a rooftop with a lake view.
  • Ristorante La Terrazza, Ascona: On the rooftop of the Hotel Casa Berno.
  • La Fontana Ristorante & Bar, Locarno: Large terrace surrounded by palm trees and Mediterranean greenery.
  • BLU Restaurant & Lounge, Locarno: Right on Locarno’s lakefront, in a relaxed atmosphere.

Renting on Lake Maggiore

Each entry links to the operator’s own website when there is one. Hours and prices change: check with them.

  • Züllig Boats, Ascona, near Ascona: On the Ascona waterfront: motorboats and pedal boats for up to 4 people.
  • Wilson SUP Center, Locarno (May to September), near Locarno – Bagno pubblico: Stand-up paddleboards to rent at the public baths in Locarno and Ascona, May to September, with hours that vary through the season.
  • Watersports.ch, Tenero, near Tenero – Lido Mappo: At Tenero lido: canoes and stand-up paddleboards to rent, boat trips, wakeboarding and banana boat.
  • Tino Noleggio Barche, Muralto, near Lido Locarno: Stand-up paddleboards, pedal boats, motorboats and jet skis to rent in Muralto, near Locarno.
