Where to swim in Lake Baldegg: every beach

By the My Lake team · published 12 November 2026 · updated 13 November 2026

In short

My Lake follows 2 beaches on Lake Baldegg, with the water temperature 70 cm below the surface where it is calculated. Here is the list, then useful places around the water.

Lake Baldegg covers about 5.2 km², is up to 66 m deep and lies at 463 m above sea level.

The 2 beaches followed on Lake Baldegg

In brackets: the morning temperature on 14 November 2026, 70 cm below the surface (Alplakes model).

  • Gelfingen (11.5 °C)
  • Baldegg (11.5 °C)

For this lake the water is a single value for the whole lake: all beaches show the same figure.

Entry, season and access

Prices and dates collected on 13 November 2026 from the sources below, for the 2026 season: check each baths’ website before you go.

  • Seebad Baldegg: 2026 season from 10 May to 13 September; adult entry CHF 6, CHF 3 for children born 2010 to 2020, free for children born from 2021. Closing times differ between sources: see the bath’s website.
  • Seebad Gelfingen: free entry, always accessible, supervised only part of the time (badi-info.ch); lawn, 3 m diving tower, raft, barbecue spot, changing rooms and toilets.

A drink or a meal by Lake Baldegg

  • Seebad Baldegg, Baldegg: Restaurant with a terrace and kiosk; free entry for restaurant-only visitors; a few minutes’ walk from the station.
