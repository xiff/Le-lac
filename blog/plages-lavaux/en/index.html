Where to swim in Lavaux

By the My Lake team · published 21 November 2026

In short

Lavaux is a wine-growing region of the canton of Vaud, known for its terraced vineyards on Lake Geneva, listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site on 28 June 2007 (898 hectares). My Lake follows 5 beaches there: Lutry, Villette, Cully, Rivaz and Saint-Saphorin.

Lavaux stretches between Lutry and Vevey. The app’s beaches in this region:

The beaches

Water temperature on the morning of 22 November 2026, from the Alplakes model: 10.1 °C to 10.2 °C depending on the beach. It changes every day.

  • Lutry (10.1 °C)
  • Villette (10.2 °C)
  • Cully – Moratel (10.1 °C)
  • Rivaz (10.2 °C)
  • Saint-Saphorin (10.2 °C)

Where to have a drink or eat by the water

  • Le Rivage, Lutry: Terrace on the Lutry quays, lake fish, at the foot of Lavaux.

Renting on the water or warming up in a sauna

Each entry links to the operator’s own website when there is one. Hours and prices change: check with them.

  • Lutry’s Paddle School, Lutry, near Lutry: Paddle boards and kayaks to rent in summer on Curtinaux beach, in the village centre.

Before you dive in: check the water quality. In winter: winter swimming on Lake Geneva. The current wind and water are in the My Lake app.
