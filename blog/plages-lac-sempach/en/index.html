Where to swim in Lake Sempach: every beach

By the My Lake team · published 11 November 2026

In short

My Lake follows 4 beaches on Lake Sempach, with the water temperature 70 cm below the surface where it is calculated. Here is the list, then useful places around the water.

Lake Sempach covers about 14.5 km², is up to 87 m deep and lies at 504 m above sea level.

The 4 beaches followed on Lake Sempach

In brackets: the morning temperature on 12 November 2026, 70 cm below the surface (Alplakes model).

  • Schenkon (11.5 °C)
  • Nottwil (11.5 °C)
  • Eich (11.5 °C)
  • Sempach (11.5 °C)

For this lake the water is a single value for the whole lake: all beaches show the same figure.

A drink or a meal by Lake Sempach

  • Badi-Restaurant Seebad Nottwil, Nottwil: Right by the lake: cold drinks, coffee and food during the bathing season; free admission.
