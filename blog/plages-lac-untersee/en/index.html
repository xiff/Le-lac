Where to swim in Lake Constance – Untersee: every beach

By the My Lake team · published 12 November 2026 · updated 13 November 2026

In short

My Lake follows 4 beaches on Lake Constance – Untersee, with the water temperature 70 cm below the surface where it is calculated. Here is the list, then useful places around the water.

Lake Constance – Untersee covers about 63 km², is up to 45 m deep and lies at 395 m above sea level.

The 4 beaches followed on Lake Constance – Untersee

In brackets: the morning temperature on 14 November 2026, 70 cm below the surface (Alplakes model).

  • Berlingen (11.1 °C)
  • Mannenbach (11.1 °C)
  • Ermatingen (11.1 °C)
  • Triboltingen (11.1 °C)

For this lake the water is a single value for the whole lake: all beaches show the same figure.

Entry, season and access

Prices and dates collected on 13 November 2026 from the sources below, for the 2026 season: check each baths’ website before you go.

  • Strandbad Ermatingen: free entry; 2026 season from 10 May to 20 September, open 10am to 10pm according to badi-info.ch (the municipality’s page only gives the 2025 season, also free).

A drink or a meal by Lake Constance – Untersee

  • Seehotel Schiff, Mannenbach: Right on the shore, 100 m from the station: big terrace and conservatory.
  • Restaurant Krone, Ermatingen: Half-timbered house with a view over the Untersee.
  • Strandbad Ermatingen, Ermatingen (May to September): Badi Beizli on the Untersee, free admission; open depending on the weather, 10 May to 20 September in 2026.

Saunas on Lake Constance – Untersee

Each entry links to the operator’s own website when there is one. Hours and prices change: check with them.

  • Bodensee-Sauna Eschenz, Eschenz (December to February), near Berlingen: Private sauna at the Buebebadi in Eschenz on the Untersee, up to six people: 1 December 2026 to 28 February 2027, Monday–Thursday 4–9pm, Friday–Sunday 10am–9pm.
