Lake Maggiore water temperature: today’s value and where it comes from

By the My Lake team · published 9 November 2026

In short

My Lake shows Lake Maggiore’s water temperature 70 cm below the surface, updated every three hours from Eawag’s Alplakes model. For this lake the model gives a single value for the whole lake: the 7 beaches we follow all show the same figure.

On 14 November 2026, in the morning, the water was at 15.4 °C for the whole lake (70 cm below the surface, Alplakes model).

Where does the Lake Maggiore temperature come from?

The figures come from Alplakes, the platform run by Eawag (the Swiss Federal Institute of Aquatic Science and Technology). It combines numerical lake models with satellite data to calculate water temperature at different depths. The data is published under a CC BY 4.0 licence, and My Lake reads it every three hours.

For Lake Maggiore, Alplakes provides a one-dimensional model (Simstrat): it calculates temperature by depth, but not by location. My Lake reads the value at 70 cm depth and shows it for every beach on the lake.

Is the temperature the same at every beach?

In the app, yes, because the model gives only one value for the lake. In reality, not necessarily: near a river mouth, in a shallow bay or after strong wind, the water can differ from the figure shown. Read it as the lake’s overall temperature, not as a measurement taken at your beach.

Why 70 cm below the surface?

Because that is roughly the water you swim in. The very top film changes quickly with sun and air; slightly deeper, the value is steadier and closer to what you feel when you get in.
