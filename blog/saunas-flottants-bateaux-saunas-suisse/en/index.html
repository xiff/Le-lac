Floating saunas and sauna boats in Switzerland: where to board

By the My Lake team · published 8 November 2026

In short

My Lake lists 6 floating saunas on four Swiss lakes: Lake Geneva, Lake Biel, Lake Zurich and Lake Lucerne. They are boats or rafts fitted with a sauna, rented by time slot for a small group, from which you jump straight into the lake.

What is a floating sauna?

A sauna built on a boat or a raft. Depending on the operator, you steer it yourself or it stays moored; the entries below say so when the operator does, along with group size and rental conditions.

The places, lake by lake

Lake Geneva

  • Sauna Boats Genève, Genève, near Plage des Eaux-Vives: Lake Geneva’s first floating sauna: up to six people, no licence needed in the harbour, departs Quai Gustave-Ador 33.

Lake Biel

  • Saunafloss Erlach, Cerlier (Erlach), near Cerlier (Erlach): Wood-fired sauna rafts moored at the Erlach landing stage on Lake Biel: straight into the lake afterwards. By booking.
  • Saunaboot Biel, Bienne (Vingelz), near Bienne – Plage: Sauna boat on Lake Biel, run by Joran-Biel (Neuenburgstrasse 72, bus stop Räblus Vingelz).

Lake Zurich

  • Saunabooten Hildi & Alice (Bootsvermietung Enge), Zurich, near Zurich – Strandbad Mythenquai: Two sauna boats at Mythenquai, rented in fixed 4-hour slots for 2 to 6 people, no licence needed (speed limited to 10 km/h), booked online. Prices vary by source: see the website.
  • Saunaboot Leopoldd, Jona (Rapperswil-Jona), near Rapperswil: Sauna boat leaving from the KIBAG marina in Jona, up to six people, 3, 4, 6 or 9-hour slots from CHF 399, no licence needed, minimum age 18.

Lake Lucerne

  • Saunaboot s’Berti, Lucerne (Kastanienbaum), near Lucerne – Lido: Sauna boat on Lake Lucerne, moored in the harbour at the foot of the Seehotel Kastanienbaum.

Entries checked on 11 November 2026. Opening hours, prices and seasons change: each sauna’s link goes to its own website, worth checking before you set off.
