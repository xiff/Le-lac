Where these figures come from

The water temperature at Préverenges comes from the Alplakes model by Eawag, which computes Lake Geneva in three dimensions: we read the value 70 cm below the surface, in front of the beach, every three hours. Wind and air come from Open-Meteo. Bathing water quality comes from the Canton of Geneva’s tests for Geneva beaches, and elsewhere from CIPEL’s annual classification for the nearest classified beach. The verdict is computed by the My Lake Today app from this data. For guidance only: the lake always has the last word.
