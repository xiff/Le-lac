Where these figures come from

The water temperature at Steinach comes from the Alplakes model by Eawag, which computes Lake Constance along its depth: one value at 70 cm for the whole lake, every three hours. Wind and air come from Open-Meteo. Bathing water quality is only shown when a test less than 30 days old exists (Canton of Geneva for Geneva beaches); otherwise check it at the source with the canton or CIPEL, links at the bottom of the page. The verdict is computed by the My Lake app from this data. For guidance only: the lake always has the last word.
