Privacy

In two sentences: monlac.ch has no account, no cookie and no ads, and keeps your choices in your browser. A few outside services receive your IP address when a page loads; here is which ones and why.

Who is responsible

monlac.ch (“My Lake”) is an independent information site with no ads and no subscription. For any question about your data: contact@monlac.ch.

What we don’t do

  • No account, no sign-up, no newsletter.
  • No cookies, no ads, no tracking across sites.
  • No user database: we cannot find you, nor your swim log.

What stays on your device

This information is saved in your browser (localStorage) and never sent to us: your beach, language, activities, favourites, your swim log (date, beach, water temperature), a few display settings (notices already read, date of your last export) and a counter that varies the headline wording.

To erase it: remove a swim with the “×” in “My history”, or clear the site’s data in your browser settings. If you do, your log is gone and we cannot recover it: export it from time to time.

Your location

“Find my beach” works out the nearest beach inside your browser, only when you press the button. Your location is never sent: the weather is requested with the beach’s coordinates.

Suggesting a place

The “A bar or restaurant is missing?” form sends nothing itself: it opens your email app with a pre-filled message. If you send it, we receive your email address and your text, only to review your suggestion.

Audience measurement: Umami

To know how many people use the site, we use Umami Cloud (Umami Software, Inc.), without cookies. According to Umami’s official documentation, it records the page viewed, referrer, browser, system, device type, language and an approximate location (country, region, city) derived from the IP address. The IP address is not kept in the statistics: together with the browser, it is used to compute an anonymous visit identifier that changes every month. It may, however, appear in the technical logs of Umami and its providers.

Umami uses subprocessors in Europe and the United States (hosting, database, content delivery); official list. We measure traffic on the basis of our legitimate interest in knowing whether the site is useful.

Outside services called by your browser

  • Open-Meteo (OpenMeteo GmbH, Bürglen UR, Switzerland) provides the weather. Your browser sends it the beach’s coordinates and, as with any request, your IP address. Open-Meteo states it uses no cookies and keeps its logs, which may contain coordinates, for 90 days. Terms and privacy.
  • existenz.ch (Christian Studer, Bureau für digitale Existenz) is only a fallback, for the lake temperature at Geneva when our model has no value. It relays data from the Swiss Federal Office for the Environment. Your IP address reaches it with the request; we found no published privacy statement for this service. Overview.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States) hosts the site. GitHub processes visitors’ IP addresses, and states it covers transfers from Switzerland and the European Union with standard contractual clauses and the Data Privacy Framework. Privacy statement.

Water data (Alplakes, Eawag), water quality (CIPEL, canton of Geneva) and other sources are read by our servers, not by your browser. Links to outside sites load only if you click.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction or erasure of your data, or object to its processing, by writing to contact@monlac.ch. Since we keep no user file, such requests mostly concern emails received. You may also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (Switzerland) or the data protection authority of your country (in France, the CNIL).

Changes

If these practices change, this page and the date below are updated.
